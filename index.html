About Your Physiotherapist

Dr. Aqib Zia — Clinical Physiotherapist

I am Dr. Aqib Zia, an experienced physiotherapist from Lahore, dedicated to helping people overcome pain, movement limitations and functional difficulties so they can return to healthier, more independent lives. My approach combines clinical assessment, individualized rehabilitation and patient-centered care rather than a one-size-fits-all routine.

At AZ Physiotherapy, every rehabilitation plan is designed around the patient's condition, goals, stage of recovery and everyday needs. Whether you are recovering after surgery, dealing with an orthopedic or sports injury, managing a neurological condition, or looking for convenient physiotherapy at home, the aim is to make recovery structured, practical and meaningful.

My goal is not simply to treat a painful area. It is to understand what is limiting your movement and function, address the underlying rehabilitation needs, build strength and confidence, and guide you toward a safer return to normal activities.
